**Things I've built**

- [**alpha-lab**](https://github.com/kristenharim/alpha-lab): a research and paper-trading platform built to kill fake alpha. Its featured case study traces a Sharpe-3.8 stat-arb result to a P&L definition you couldn't actually trade, then retires it.
- [**catalyst-integrity-desk**](https://github.com/kristenharim/catalyst-integrity-desk): flags when a biotech thesis rests on trial dates that have quietly expired. IBM AI Builders Challenge entry.
- [**Rumi**](https://github.com/IBS27/rumi): an AI agent that turns a room scan and a budget into a 3D room of real products. HackMIT 2026: 1st of 128 in the Long Lake challenge, 2nd in Ramp's.
