### Hi, I'm Kristen!

I'm an applied math student at UC Berkeley (class of 2028). Right now I do factor-model research with Prof. Lisa Goldberg at Berkeley's Consortium for Data Analytics in Risk (CDAR), where I study how far you can trust PCA-estimated risk factors when there are more assets than observations. Before that I did CRISPR research in Jennifer Doudna's lab and deeptech diligence at IndieBio.

**Some things I've built**

- [**factor-trust**](https://github.com/kristenharim/factor-trust): a Streamlit tool that scores how much to trust each estimated factor direction, with the asymptotic error floor, Monte Carlo error bands and an asset-budget sweep.
- [**alpha-lab**](https://github.com/kristenharim/alpha-lab): a research and paper-trading platform for catching alpha that isn't real. The case study I'm proudest of traces a Sharpe-3.8 stat-arb result to a P&L definition you couldn't actually trade, then retires it.
- [**catalyst-integrity-desk**](https://github.com/kristenharim/catalyst-integrity-desk): flags when a biotech thesis rests on trial dates that have quietly expired. Built for the IBM AI Builders Challenge.
- [**Rumi**](https://github.com/IBS27/rumi): an AI agent that turns a room scan and a budget into a 3D room of real products, built with three teammates at HackMIT 2026 (1st of 128 in the Long Lake challenge, 2nd in Ramp's).

Outside of research, I co-organize weekly poker tournaments between Berkeley and Stanford.
